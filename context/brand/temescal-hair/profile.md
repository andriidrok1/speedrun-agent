# Temescal Hair Co.: brand profile

> **Everything here is fictional.** Temescal Hair Co. does not exist. The salon, the owner, the prices, the campaign and the past deals were invented for the hackathon demo. Any resemblance to a real Oakland salon is accidental.

## Who they are

Owner-run salon on Telegraph Avenue in Oakland's Temescal district. Six chairs, five stylists plus the owner, Dani Reyes, who still cuts four days a week. Open since 2019. Known for lived-in color (balayage and glosses that grow out without a hard line, so clients return every 10 to 12 weeks instead of every 5) and curly cuts done dry, curl by curl.

Retail: Signature Cut + Style $95, Lived-in Color Session $260, Curly Cut + Treatment $140, Gloss + Blowout $120. Cost to the shop (stylist time plus product) is about a third of retail.

## Why they sponsor creators

Bookings. A view from someone in Phoenix is worth nothing to a salon with six chairs. Dani wants a woman in Oakland, Berkeley or SF who sees a creator in the chair, thinks "that's the color I keep screenshotting," and taps the booking link. Fall is the slow stretch, so the push is about filling October with first-timers who become regulars.

Cash is tight. An empty chair on a Tuesday costs almost nothing to fill, so paying in services is close to free for the shop and worth full retail to the creator. That asymmetry is the whole strategy.

## Brand voice

Warm, direct, a little cheeky. Talks like a stylist who has heard every hair horror story and isn't fazed. Never says "luxury" or "transformation".

- "Your color should grow out like it meant to."
- "Curls get cut dry here. That's just how you see the shape."
- "Come as you are, leave with hair you don't have to think about for three months."

## Content guidelines

- Film in the chair, in the shop. The salon on screen is the point.
- Show the service happening: foils, the cut, the blowout. Before and after welcome.
- Name the stylist. Clients book people, not shops.
- Tag @temescalhair, link sticker to the booking page, FTC disclosure.
- Creator's own words, no script.

## No-gos

- No "before" shots mocking a previous stylist or another salon by name.
- No first-visit discount codes above 15%.
- No content filmed outside the salon presented as the salon's work.
- No claims ("damage-free", "permanent") the shop can't back up.
- No perpetual usage rights. Organic plus 30-day paid reposts is the ceiling.

## How the negotiator behaves (persona)

Dani negotiates herself, by DM, friendly and on first names from the start. Cash-poor, service-rich. She opens with services (a color session, a cut, maybe a standing blowout) and throws in the salon's Instagram feature and event-night early access unasked, since they cost her almost nothing. Cash comes last and in small steps. She opens at 60% of her private per-creator ceiling, adds up to 15% per round, six rounds, then politely walks. What she cares about most is that the creator actually sits in the chair and gets the service on camera. Cash-only asks or filming at home are a mismatch, and she says so early. She trades a higher booking-referral percentage (up to 15%) for cash every time.

## Past deals (made up)

1. **Spring 2026, Berkeley lifestyle creator, 28k IG.** Lived-in Color Session plus Gloss + Blowout ($380 retail), $150 cash, feature on the salon's Instagram. 3 Stories and 1 Reel from the chair. 41 booking-link taps, 9 new clients, 6 rebooked.
2. **Summer 2026, Oakland curly-hair creator, 12k IG + 35k TikTok.** No cash. Curly Cut + Treatment plus a standing monthly blowout for 3 months ($500 retail), 12% booking referral. 1 TikTok and 2 Stories. 14 new curly-cut clients; she still posts from the chair unpaid.
